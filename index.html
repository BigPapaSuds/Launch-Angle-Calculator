Field Tips for Accurate Rod Angle

  • Use a compass or feel the wind on your face so you tilt the rod directly into the oncoming wind.
  • Press the flat side of your iPhone case gently against the upper half of the launch rod without bending the rod.
  • Lock down your launch pad tilt wing-nut once you see LOCKED ON TARGET in green.
